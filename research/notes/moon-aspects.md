# The Moon aspected by the planets (Brihat Jataka XIX)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, chapter XIX "On Planetary Aspects", verses 1-9 (pp. 175-180),
and II.13 for the aspects. **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-moon-aspects.yaml`, **unchecked**; quoted, scan errors corrected ("peison" -> person,
"bon king" -> become a king, "Vernus" -> Venus, "foud of the wrives" -> fond of the wives, "diunkard"-style slips). Shown inside the **chart:moon** answer.

**Used:** the Moon in each of the 12 signs and in each of the 7 navamsa lords' navamsas (Mars, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Sun, Jupiter, Saturn),
aspected by each of Sun, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn; the bot finds which planets aspect the Moon by II.13's **full** sights (every planet
the 7th; Saturn the 3rd and 10th; Jupiter the 5th and 9th; Mars the 4th and 8th) and prints the matching rows. Verse 9's rule for how much comes
to pass (full, imperfect or small by vargottama / own / other navamsa and benefic or malefic aspect) is quoted in the More view, not computed.

**Cross-check with Phaladeepika 18.6-15 (Kapoor):** the six-planet lists agree with Brihat Jataka wherever the translation's list is complete. It
drops an item in six places (Aries, Leo, Virgo, Scorpio and two more are missing one of the six), so it is not used as a second source.

## Left out
- **The partial sights** (quarter, half and three-quarters): the Moon is shown aspected only by full sights.
- **Hora, drekkana and dwadasamsa** versions (verse 4 and its notes, and Phaladeepika 18.16): the same table applied to those divisions; not computed.
- **The Sun and the rising sign read the same way** (verse 8 note and verse 3 note): not built; the table is for the Moon.
- **Whether the aspecting planet is benefic or malefic by hora/navamsa** (the notes to verse 4): no.
