# Brihat Jataka XXIII: malefic yogas (verses 1-17)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 193-200, read in full; scan errors checked against the Sanskrit (`research/sources/gretil-brhajjataka.txt`, BJ_23). **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-malefic-yogas.yaml`, **unchecked**; 49 rules, each with a plain `definition` for the checker.
Topic `chart:malefic` ("what malefic yogas do I have"). **SENSITIVE: behind the consent gate for everyone, with the stronger warning for a child.** It speaks of "a person's wife" because it was written for a man; the frame says so.

**Used:** sons and wife by benefics or lords in the 5th and 7th from the lagna or Moon (and the translator's "otherwise" reading); the Virgo-lagna wife and sons rule; the Moon and Saturn in the 7th; the number of wives; a single wife and several wives; the wife's death by fire, a fall or ropes;
one eye; defective limbs (Venus with the Sun); a barren wife; neither wife nor sons (twice); adultery (twice); a late marriage; the destroyed family; a serving woman's son; deeds unsuited to rank; swelling illness; genital disease; white leprosy; defective limbs (twice); asthma and consumption; pulmonary consumption; the skeleton; leprosy (twice);
blindness and deafness (with the translator's causes by the strongest planet); deformed teeth; windy complaints; insanity; idiocy (twice); servitude by navamsa; ugly teeth; baldness; weak sight; various diseases; imprisonment (two rules, with the translator's kinds by rising sign); the servant by the malefics in the 10th.

**Judgment calls (for the checker):**
- *Malefic planets:* Sun, Mars, Saturn throughout; the Moon is added only where the translator names "the Sun, the Moon, Mars and Saturn" (the blind, the deaf, the imprisoned, 23.10, 23.11, 23.16; "in any way" is read as one planet in each of the four houses, in any order). Rahu and Ketu are not used in this chapter.
- *The wife's death (23.2)* carries a proviso only in the translator's note (an Agni, Nipata or Pasa drekkana for Venus). It is required; without it "ropes" would fire for any Venus without a benefic, in about a third of charts. The drekkanas are read from chapter XXVII's figures: Agni = those that "carry fire" (Taurus 1st, Aquarius 2nd); Nipata = the figure about to fall (Libra 2nd) or standing on a pit's brink (Pisces 3rd); Pasa = the figure in fetters and a nose string, which the 23.16 note names as the 1st of Capricorn. **The Nipata reading is the least certain.**
- *The Sarpa drekkanas (23.16)*: read from chapter XXVII's figures that carry a snake: Cancer 3rd, Scorpio 1st and 2nd, Pisces 3rd. **The translator's notes disagree:** 23.16's note says the 1st of Cancer, 1st and 2nd of Scorpio, 3rd of Pisces (the first is probably a misprint for the third); chapter XXI's note (used in the Moon's drekkana rule of `chart:vargas`) says the 2nd of Cancer, the 1st of Scorpio and the 3rd of Pisces.
  Chapter XXI's list was kept as printed. **Open question for the checker:** align them.
- *Chapter XXI's Ayudha list also differs* from chapter XXVII's "armed" figures; kept as printed.
- *Verse 8 "Sun in the sign or navamsa of the Moon":* the translation is followed; the Sanskrit reads as the Sun and Moon each in the other's sign or navamsa.
- *"Most powerful of the four" (blind, deaf):* the planet with the best standing in its sign (exaltation, moolatrikona, own, friendly, neutral, inimical, depression); ties list every tied planet. Not the six strengths of Phaladeepika 4.
- *"Varga of Saturn or Mars" (adultery):* a sign or a navamsa of Saturn or Mars.
- *23.5 male and female planets:* male = Sun, Jupiter; female = Moon, Venus (Mercury and Saturn neuter), sharing a sign.
- *The first rule of each pair (sons, wife)* fires for nearly every chart, and the "will not have" form for those with no support; the frame warns that some rules fire widely.
- *23.3:* "the 12th and the 6th" (the Sanskrit's vyaya, ari); Iyer's note quotes a variant (Moon for Venus) that is not coded.

**Left out:** the painter (23.6, the rendering cannot be coded: the Sanskrit reads as a kendra Saturn aspecting a drekkana joined with Mercury), the eclipsed Moon (ghosts) and eclipsed Sun (loss of eyes) of 23.12 (need an eclipse at birth, which is not computed), the Moon with a halo (23.17 first half: needs the halo), and the Yavana variant on leprosy.
