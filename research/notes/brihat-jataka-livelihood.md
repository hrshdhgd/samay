# Brihat Jataka X: livelihood (verses 1-4)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 110-113, read in full. **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-livelihood.yaml`, **unchecked**; quoted with the translator's notes.
Shown inside the existing career answer (`life:career`), not as a new topic.

**Used:** the planet in the 10th from the lagna and from the Moon names the source of wealth (Sun father, Moon mother, Mars enemy, Mercury friend, Jupiter brother, Venus wife, Saturn servant; both sources if both 10ths are occupied);
if neither is occupied, the work is read from the navamsa lord of the 10th lord from the lagna, from the Moon and from the Sun (the translator says all three); verse 4's conditions (the planet in a friend's, enemy's or own sign; the Sun exalted;
powerful benefics in the 11th, the lagna or the 2nd).

**Judgment calls:** the fallback applies only when no planet is in either 10th (verse 1: "if there be no such planet"). "Powerful" benefics in verse 4 is approximated as Mercury, Jupiter or Venus standing in the 11th, 1st or 2nd (no measure of power).
The yoga planet's "friendly or inimical sign" uses the chapter 20 friendships.
