# Brihat Jataka XXII: miscellaneous yogas (verses 1-6)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 191-193, read in full. **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-miscellaneous.yaml`, **unchecked**; quoted, translator's notes applied.
Shown inside the yogas answer (`chart:yogas`) as a fifth source, hidden until its own file is checked.

**Used:** karaka (helping) planets (22.1-3): planets in own, exaltation or moolatrikona signs and in kendras from the lagna, the special karaka in the 10th from another, the planets in the 4th and 10th to a planet in the rising sign, the commentator's kendra-pair inference, and verse 3's moolatrikona/exaltation planet with the planet in the 10th from it
and that planet's atimitra; the happiness yogas (22.4): vargottama rising navamsa, vargottama Moon, benefics in the 2nd from the Sun, occupied kendras (the translator's one/two/benefic gradations), karaka planets present; Jupiter or the lord of the Moon's sign or of the lagna in a kendra (22.5);
the sign class (Prishtodaya, Sirodaya, Sira-Prishtodaya, from I.10) of each planet's natal sign and so where in its dasha its effects fall (22.5); the transit-within-a-sign rule (22.6) is in the file but not shown, since it needs a live transit.

**Judgment calls (for the checker):**
- *The scan of stanza 2 is missing.* Its rule (planets in the 4th and 10th are karakas to a planet in the rising sign, not the reverse) comes from the translator's note, cited as "22.2 (note)".
- *Verse 1 needs at least two* dignified kendra planets to be "karaka to one another"; the book does not say what one alone is.
- *The kendra-pair inference* is the commentator's (note a), not the stanza's; it fires for dignified planets in kendras from each other but not both in kendras from the lagna (that is verse 1).
- *Atimitra* is read as Phaladeepika 2.21-23 defines it (natural friend and temporary friend), the same code as chapter 7.23.
- *"Jupiter, the lord of the Moon's sign, or the lord of the ascendant"* is read as any one of them in a kendra; the answer names which.
- *The dasha timing* is shown for each of the seven planets from its natal sign (the book speaks of the lord at the commencement of the period); Rahu and Ketu are left out, since the text names no class for them.
- *Gemini* is a Sirodaya sign (translator's note to I.10: it is the exception to the night signs rising by the feet).
- *Left out:* the Moon's passage through a karaka's house as a timing (Yogayatra, from the note): it needs a transit; shown only as the translator's remark.
