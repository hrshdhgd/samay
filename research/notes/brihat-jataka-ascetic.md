# Brihat Jataka XV: ascetic yogas (verses 1-4)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 155-157, read in full. **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-ascetic.yaml`, **unchecked**; engine `samay/engine/ascetic_bj.py`.
Shown inside `chart:yogas` as a sixth source (hidden until its own file is checked). No consent gate.

**Used:** the seven ascetic orders by the most powerful planet of four or more planets in a sign (15.1, with the translator's description of each order); the combust case (15.2); the lord of the Moon's sign aspecting Saturn, Saturn aspecting a weak lord, and the Moon in Saturn's drekkana with a Saturn or Mars navamsa aspected by Saturn (15.3); the scientific author and the king-and-ascetic (15.4, each needing a raja yoga, counted from chapter 11 of this book and chapter 7 of Phaladeepika), with the translator's note on how raja and ascetic yogas weigh.

**Judgment calls (for the checker):**
- *"Powerful" planets (15.1):* read as in Phaladeepika 27.2 (already built): four or more planets in one sign, the best standing in the sign gives the order. Requiring each to be in dignity can never happen. The translator's note says five, six or seven; the text's four is used.
- *The reversion (15.1, "defeated in conjunction", planetary war):* **left out**; the code has no planetary-war rule. Also left out: 15.2's second sentence (a defeated planet aspected by others).
- *15.3 "not aspected by any other planet":* full aspects of II.13, conjunction not counted.
- *Raja yoga (15.4):* any yoga from `raja_yogas` or `phaladeepika_raja` counts; the two-raja-yogas-make-a-king remark is quoted, not coded.
