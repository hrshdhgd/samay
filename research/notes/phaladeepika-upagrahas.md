# Gulika and the other upagrahas (Phaladeepika 25)

**Source:** Mantreswara's *Phala Deepika*, tr. G. S. Kapoor (in copyright), pp. 241-246, read in full. **File:** `data/rules/phaladeepika-ch25-upagrahas.yaml`, **unchecked**, paraphrased.
Topic **chart:upagrahas** ("where is my Gulika"). For a child, gated like health (the Gulika texts speak of short life and disease).

**Computed (the new thing):** Gulika/Mandi, Yamakantaka, Ardhaprahara and Kala are the *lagna at a moment* fixed by the book's tables: the ghatika (of a 30-ghatika day from sunrise, or night
from sunset, scaled in proportion) at whose end the point stands; the lagna is then worked out for that moment and place. Dhuma, Vyatipata, Paridhi, Indra Dhanus and Upaketu come from the Sun's
longitude by the book's formulas. **The formulas reproduce the book's own worked example exactly** (a test); the timed points are tested for the weekday rule (a day birth uses that day's
sunrise weekday; a night birth the weekday of the sunset it follows, so a birth before dawn belongs to the day before).

**Shown:** each point's degree, sign and house; Gulika's house (25.8-14), Gulika with each planet in its sign (25.15-17), Upaketu's house (25.26), the nature of the others (25.22-25), the
general lines (25.18-21), and verse 30 when the lord of Gulika's house is well placed.

## Judgment calls
- The tables' numbers are the book's, which fall by 4 ghatikas a day; they differ a little from the modern "Saturn's eighth of the day" method (the book's values are used as printed, scaled to the
  day or night length). The misprinted "13" in Kala's row is read as 18 (the row falls by 4).
- The book gives night values for Mandi only; for a night birth the other three timed points are not worked out, and the answer says so.
- Verse 30's "strong lord" is read as: a kendra or trikona, or an own, moolatrikona, exaltation or friendly sign.
- **Yamakantaka** is called good in 25.18 and 25.21 and yet 25.23 lists danger from horned animals for it "in the same position". Both are shown as the book gives them.

## Left out
25.17 (Gulika's time coinciding with *tyajyakala*), 25.14's last line (the lagna or Moon a trikona from Gulika), 25.22 (houses conjoined with an upagraha are destroyed: only the shown nature is used),
25.27-29 (the five are invisible or omens in the sky), and the effects of the upagrahas in the houses "in the chapter on houses" (chapter 26 verses on Gulika: not yet read).
