# Brihat Jataka XXIV: the horoscopy of women (verses 1-16)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 201-207, read in full; scan errors checked against the Sanskrit (BJ_24 in `gretil-brhajjataka.txt`). **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-women.yaml`, **unchecked**; 32 coded rules plus the trimsamsa table (7 groups x 5 lords) and the setting-lord table.
Shown inside `chart:women` as a second source after Phaladeepika 11, so it is **behind the consent gate** (stronger warning for a child). Each source hides until its own file is checked.

**Used:** the male or female character from the odd or even lagna and Moon signs (24.2 and the translator's mixed case); the rising trimsamsa and the Moon's trimsamsa read with their sign (24.3-5), the more powerful lord's effect only (24.6); Saturn and Venus in each other's navamsa (24.7);
the husband from the 7th from the lagna and from the Moon: weak and empty, Mercury, movable/fixed/common sign (translator), Sun, Mars or Saturn with malefic aspect, several malefics, mixed, weak with a benefic aspect (24.8-9); Venus and Mars in each other's navamsa, the Moon with both (24.9);
the lagna's Saturn/Mars sign with Moon and Venus (24.10); the setting navamsa (24.10) and the lord of the setting sign or navamsa for the husband's nature (24.11-12, the stronger of the two when they differ, the translator's note); planets in the lagna (24.13); the 8th house, widowhood and its antardasha, the 2nd (24.14);
the masculine and the famed combinations by strength (24.15); the ascetic (24.16).

**Judgment calls (for the checker):**
- "Weak" 7th (24.8-9): its lord in an inimical or depression sign or in the 6th, 8th or 12th; "powerful/weak" planets in 24.15: strong = own, moolatrikona, exaltation; weak = inimical or depression; Saturn "neither" = friendly or neutral.
- The 7th is read from the lagna and from the Moon (24.8-9 say "from the rising sign or from the sign occupied by the Moon"); the detail names which.
- 24.7's "each other's navamsa" = Saturn in a navamsa of Venus (Taurus or Libra) and Venus in one of Saturn (Capricorn or Aquarius), plus mutual aspect. 24.9's Venus and Mars likewise.
- The setting navamsa is the navamsa of the lagna longitude plus 180 degrees. Malefics: Sun, Mars, Saturn; Rahu and Ketu are not in this chapter.
- 24.15 translator's "according to some, many men" and the 24.12 variant "ratikarma" are quoted as notes only, not coded.
- The "time of query" remark (24.16) is quoted as the chapter's closing frame, not coded.

**Left out:** none of the verses; the "sons and wife" verse of chapter 23.1 is in `chart:malefic`.
