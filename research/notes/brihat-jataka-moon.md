# Brihat Jataka XVI and XVII, the Moon's nakshatra and sign: what was used and what was fixed

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka of Varaha Mihira*, Madras 1885, public domain (archive.org
b2488442x): chapter XVI "On the Nakshatras, or the Moon in the Asterisms" (pp. 157-160) and chapter XVII "On the Moon
in the Several Signs of the Zodiac" (pp. 160-164). **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-moon.yaml`, **unchecked**. The
descriptions are **quoted** from the translation (public domain), with the scan errors below corrected.

**Used:** all 27 nakshatra descriptions and all 12 sign descriptions, shown by the new topic **chart:moon** ("what is my
nakshatra", "tell me about my personality"), with the text's own conditions: the nakshatra effects come fully only if the
Moon is powerful (XVI note); the sign effects come fully only if the Moon, its sign and the sign's lord are all powerful
(XVII.13), and Iyer's note that with two of the three they come imperfectly, with one still more so, with none not at all.
The bot does not judge "powerful"; it quotes the condition. The pada is shown but the text gives nothing by pada.
