# Planets together: Brihat Jataka XIV and Phaladeepika 18

**Sources:** Brihat Jataka chapter XIV "On Double Planetary Yogas", verses 1-5 (Iyer 1885, public domain, pp. 142-144, quoted with scan
errors corrected: "tho", "bo", "ehief", "potman" kept as printed), and Phaladeepika chapter 18 verses 1-5 (Kapoor, pp. 169-170,
paraphrased). **File:** `data/rules/conjunctions.yaml`, **unchecked**. Shown by the new topic **chart:conjunctions** ("which planets
are together in my chart", "do I have any yogas"): for each sign holding two or more of the Sun to Saturn, each pair's text from
both books, with verses.

**Used:** all 21 pairs; the rule for three or more planets (read each pair in turn, both texts).

## The two texts agree in sense, but differ in places

Both give the same 21 pairs. Examples of difference, shown side by side rather than reconciled: Sun-Jupiter (BJ "cruel and will work
for other men", PD "cruel, but intent on helping others"); Mars-Venus (BJ "will protect cows", PD "owner of a court"); Moon-Mars
(BJ adds "sale of works of art, of women, of liquor"); Mercury-Jupiter (BJ "skilled in duels", PD "an actor"). BJ's translator's notes
(Punarbhu for the Moon-Saturn "son of a re-married woman"; "engines of destruction, according to the commentator") are kept with the
quote.

## Left out

- **The Jataka Parijata pairs** that the Iyer translation adds in a long note under verse 5 (two to six planets together, pp. 144-148):
  a later work, not Varahamihira, with its own results for every group of 2-6 planets. Not used; a candidate for a separate source.
- **Aspects on the Moon (BJ XIX, PD 18.6-17):** the Moon in each sign aspected by each planet, and in each navamsa. Needs the full
  aspect rule (BJ II.13: all planets aspect the 7th fully; Saturn the 3rd and 10th, Jupiter the 5th and 9th, Mars the 4th and 8th) and
  navamsa friendship; 12 signs x 6 planets and 7 navamsas x 6 planets. Not yet built.
