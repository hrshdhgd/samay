# Brihat Jataka XXVII: the 36 drekkana figures (verses 1-36)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 225-233, read in full. **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-drekkanas.yaml`, **unchecked**; engine `samay/engine/drekkana_figures.py`.
Shown inside `chart:lagna` as the figure of the rising drekkana (verse number, lord, the translator's shape note, the frame). Not a new topic; no consent gate (descriptive, not about death).

**Used:** all 36 figures, quoted with scan errors corrected, and the translator's shape notes. The lord is not stored: it is computed as the lord of the sign, the 5th or the 9th from it (I.11), and agrees with every lord the translator names.

**Judgment calls (for the checker):**
- Verse 2 (Aries 2nd) opens "According to Yavanacharya"; kept in the text.
- Verse 34 (Pisces 1st) has no shape note in the scan; shown without one.
- Only the rising drekkana is shown; the Moon's and the planets' drekkana figures are not (the book does not say what they mean). The translator's Yogayatra remark (agreeable figures = success, hideous or armed = disgrace, serpents or fetters = delirium and imprisonment) is in the frame, not coded.
- The same figures define the Sarpa, Agni, Nipata and Pasa drekkanas used in chapters XXIII and XXV; those sets are still read from them by hand (see those notes).

**Left out:** the rest of chapter XXVII's remarks (use in horary astrology, tracing thieves).
