**Built:** 17.2-6, 9, 10, 11 (the Sun's sign, "the month"), 12 (Jupiter in Mandi's navamsa, Saturn in its dwadasamsa), 13-16, 19-25 (day and night forms), 27, 28 (Saturn, Jupiter, Sun). Points are the book's own constructions:
sums and differences of longitudes, Gulika and Yamakantaka (from chapter 25), the 22nd decanate lord, trines.

**Many of these hold for most of the five years** (Saturn or Jupiter in one of several signs, or a trine from it, is true on more than half the days). The answer says so for those ("does not narrow anything").
That is what the book's rules give, and the translator's own note (17.4) says it: they are to be read with the dasha of a maraka planet, not as dates.

## Judgment calls
"22nd decanate" = counting the lagna's decanate as the 1st (36 decanates from Aries; the lord of a decanate by the usual rule: the sign's lord, the 5th's, the 9th's). "Trikona" = 0, 4, 8 signs.
Gulika for a night birth is as chapter 25 gives it; the day/night rules (17.21, 17.25) are run only for the birth's kind. The Moon's transit rules (17.19, 17.20) use sign only (the book also names a nakshatra).
