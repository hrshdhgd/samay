# Brihat Jataka VIII: the Kendradi life-periods (verses 1-19)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 77-88, read through stanza 21. **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-ayurdasa.yaml`, **unchecked**; engine `samay/engine/ayurdasa_bj.py`.
MOST SENSITIVE: inside `chart:longevity`, behind the consent gate (stronger for a child). This is a different system from the Vimshottari dasha the rest of the bot uses; the answer says so.

**Built:** the order (the most powerful of lagna, Sun, Moon first; then kendra, panaphara, apoklima planets from it, each by power then the longer period); the lengths (the planets' Pindayurdaya years after reductions, chapter 7); the nine names (Sampoorna, Poorna, Rikta, Anishta, Avarohini, Madhyama, Arohini, Adhama, Misraphala) with the translator's meaning and a good/bad/mixed nature; the lagna period named by the rising drekkana (8.8); each planet's good and bad period (8.12-18), a mixed period quoting both (8.19).

**Judgment calls (for the checker):**
- *Name priority:* Sampoorna (within a degree of the exaltation point), Anishta (depression degree with an inimical navamsa), Poorna (exaltation sign), Rikta (depression sign), Misraphala (the translator's note c), then Madhyama or Avarohini (between exaltation and depression going forward), Adhama or Arohini (the other way). The book's order of precedence is not stated.
- *"Most powerful":* best standing in the sign; the lagna's strength is its lord's.
- *A period's good/bad/mixed nature* comes from its name by the translator's note (Sampoorna, Poorna, Uttama good; Rikta, Anishta, Adhama bad; the rest mixed).
- *Length:* Pindayurdaya years, since the text says the lengths are those "of the last chapter"; the total can be well past 120.

**Left out (stated in the answer):** the sub-periods (8.3-4), the Naisargika periods (8.9, a second set 1, 2, 9, 20, 18, 20, 50 years), the conditions of a prosperous period (8.10), the Moon's sign at the start (8.11), and the metals, elements and complexions of 8.20-21 (the last needs Brihat Samhita).
