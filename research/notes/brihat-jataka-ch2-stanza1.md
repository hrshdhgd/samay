# Brihat Jataka II.1: what each planet stands for

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *The Brihat Jataka of Varaha Mihira*, Madras: Foster Press,
1885, chapter II, stanza 1, p. 14. Public Domain Mark on the Wellcome Library copy:
https://archive.org/details/b2488442x (full text: `b2488442x_djvu.txt`). Re-fetch into
`research/sources/` (git-ignored) to check.

**Translation text as printed** (OCR; "un sellors" is a scan error for "counsellors"):

> To the Kalapurusha, the Sun is the soul, the Moon is the mind, Mars is strength, Mercury is
> speech, Jupiter is knowledge and health, Venus is desire, and Saturn is sorrow. Of the planets the
> Sun and Moon are kings, Mars is General, Mercury is the first prince, Jupiter and Venus are
> counsellors and Saturn is servant.

**Paraphrase choices**
- "Kalapurusha" is rendered "the cosmic person (Kalapurusha)" (literally the "person of time"; the
  zodiac seen as one body). The Sanskrit word is kept in brackets at h2's request, for transparency.
- "First prince" is rendered "prince (heir to the throne)".
- Stanza 7 of the same chapter assigns castes to the planets. Deliberately not used.
- Rahu and Ketu: stanza 3 gives only their names (Rahu: Tama "darkness", Agu, Asura; Ketu: Sikhi).
  No meaning, so no entry.
