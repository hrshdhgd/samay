# Brihat Jataka VII: Ayurdaya, the length of life (verses 1-14)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 60-71, read through stanza 14. **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-ayurdaya.yaml`, **unchecked**; engine `samay/engine/ayurdaya_bj.py`.
MOST SENSITIVE (a number of years): inside `chart:longevity`, behind the consent gate (stronger for a child). Both methods are shown with their working and the book's own caveats.

**Built:** Pindayurdaya (7.1-4): years by the planet's arc from its exaltation point (full at exaltation, half at depression, linear between), lagna years = degrees x 0.3, the enemy-sign, combust, Chakrapata (12th..7th, malefics 1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5, 1/6, benefics half of that, the most powerful per sign) and Krurodaya reductions; Satyacharya's method (7.9-13): navamsas passed counted mod 12, tripled for exaltation or retrograde, doubled for vargottama, own sign or own drekkana (the largest only), same reductions except Krurodaya; the horoscope beyond calculation (7.14).
**Checked against the translator's worked example** (Aquarius rising; Sun, Moon, Venus exalted; Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn depressed; Mars 28 deg Aquarius): Mars 13.75, Mercury 6, Jupiter 7.5 halved to 3.75 in the 12th, Krurodaya leaving about 17 years (the book's 16 years and months, with its Saturn combust).

**Judgment calls (for the checker):**
- *The scan lost "one-fourth" in 7.3* ("full, one-half, one-third, one-fifth and one-sixth" for six houses); the worked examples (Mars 11th half, Saturn 8th a fifth) fix the sequence 1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5, 1/6.
- *Exaltation points:* Sun Aries 10, Moon Taurus 3, Mars Capricorn 28, Mercury Virgo 15, Jupiter Cancer 5, Venus Pisces 27, Saturn Libra 20 (stanza I.13; the examples agree).
- *Reductions are applied to each planet's own years*; "several planets in one sign" uses the best standing; Rahu and Ketu are not used; the Sun and the Moon are never reduced as combust or inimical (the Moon is, by the same test; flagged).
- *Krurodaya:* any of the Sun, Mars, Saturn in the rising sign; the navamsas counted are those before the lagna's own degree; only once if several.
- *Lagna years in Satyacharya's method:* signs passed if the lagna lord is in an own, moolatrikona or exaltation sign, else navamsas passed (mod 12).
- *The translator's note that Pinda gives under 20 years in no case* and the commentator's rebuttal are not coded; the totals are capped at 120 for display. Both methods disagree widely, and the answer says so.
- *Under eight years:* the book excludes balarishta; the answer quotes this.

**Left out:** the 5th stanza's animal lifespans; the translator's list of 28 horoscopes beyond calculation (one is coded, stanza 14 only); the Jeevasarma variant (one seventh of 120 years, "stands alone"); chapter VII's Saravali horoscope table.
