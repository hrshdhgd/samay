# Phaladeepika chapter 19 (dasa effects)

**Source:** Mantreswara's *Phala Deepika*, English translation, commentary and annotation by Dr. G. S. Kapoor
(a modern translation, so in copyright). Openly available at
http://www.panchanga.lv/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Phaladipika_Shri-Mantreswara.pdf (265 pages, clean text
layer, much better than the OCR on WisdomLib). Chapter 19 is PDF pages 174-181. The PDF and its extracted text
are in `research/sources/` (git-ignored). Per the owner's source policy these entries are **paraphrases**.
The author is believed to have lived in the 13th century (translator's preface).

**Scope. Only verses 18-26 are used.** The translator's note after verse 17 says verses 5-17 describe the
effects of the dasas "in a general way" and could apply to other dasa systems; verses 18-26 describe them "in the
order prescribed under the Vimsottari dasa system". The WisdomLib summary I read first did not separate the two.

**Agreement with samay's calculation.** Verse 2's order and years (Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16,
Saturn 19, Mercury 17, Ketu 7, Venus 20) match samay's Vimshottari. (The translator's table prints Venus as 10;
the verse and every other source say 20. A typo in the table.)

**Where the book and samay differ (not changed, recorded for the tradition-profile decisions)**
- Verse 3: Mantreswara works out the part of the first dasha left at birth by treating every birth star as 60
  ghatikas long. The translator says this is "not correct" and that the generally recognised method uses the
  star's real length. samay uses the generally recognised method (the fraction of the star the Moon has crossed).
- Verse 4: the year for dasha dates is the solar year (the Sun's return to the same zodiac point). samay uses
  365.25 days; a sidereal year is about 365.256, a difference of under a day over the whole 120-year cycle.
- The translator notes that in the Vimshottari system the effects are also governed by Parasara's rules about
  which houses a planet rules (functional benefics and malefics). samay does not compute that.

**Guardrails.** The effects are listed in general, mixing good and bad. The translator-editor stresses (notes to
verses 18, 19 and 21) that they depend on how strong and well placed the planet is. Every dasha answer therefore
ends with a fixed line saying so and that this is not a forecast.
