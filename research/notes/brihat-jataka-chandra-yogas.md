# Brihat Jataka XIII, the lunar yogas: what was used and what was left out

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, chapter XIII "On Chandra (Lunar) Yogas",
pp. 135-142, verses 1-3 and 5-9. **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-chandra-yogas.yaml`, **unchecked**. Quoted, with scan
errors corrected ("nth" -> 5th, "0th" -> 6th, "Annpha" -> Anapha, "Durudhura" for "Durudhura/Dnrudhura", "yogi" -> yoga, etc.).
Shown inside the **chart:moon** answer ("what is my nakshatra", "tell me about my personality"), gated separately from the
nakshatra and sign descriptions.

**Used (all computed from the chart, no judgement calls):**
- Verse 1: the Moon's house from the Sun, graded poor (1, 4, 7, 10), moderate (2, 5, 8, 11) or excellent (3, 6, 9, 12) for
  "conduct, wealth, knowledge, intelligence and skill" (the houses are the translator's note).
- Verse 2: Adhi yoga, Mercury, Jupiter or Venus in the 6th, 7th or 8th from the Moon.
- Verses 3, 5, 6: Sunapha, Anapha, Durudhura, Kemadruma. The planets that count are the five from Mars to Saturn (the
  translator's note); the Sun does not (verse 3), and Rahu and Ketu are not counted (the text names only the five).
- Verses 7-8: the effect of the planet that makes the yoga, for each of the five; with several, all their effects.
- Verse 9: Mercury, Jupiter and Venus in the 3rd, 6th, 10th or 11th from the ascendant and from the Moon (how many: three, two,
  one), with the text's statement that this yoga overrules a malefic one such as Kemadruma.
- Kemadruma: the text reports that "some" (Garga and Parasara) say it ceases when the Moon is in a kendra from the ascendant or
  has a planet with her. Both facts are computed for the chart and shown next to the quote; the bot does not decide.

## Left out, and why

- **Verse 1, second half:** rich and comfortable if the Moon is in her own or an Atimitra navamsa and aspected by Jupiter (by day)
  or Venus (by night). Needs aspects and navamsa friendship; not computed.
- **Verse 3, the alternative readings** (yogas counted from the kendras from the Moon, or from the navamsa sign): the text itself
  says "these views are not considered as authoritative".
- **Verse 4:** the counts of combinations (31 Sunapha, 31 Anapha, 180 Durudhura): arithmetic, not a result.
- **Verse 8, last sentence:** whether the Moon's hemisphere (above or below the horizon) brings misery or prosperity by day or
  night. Needs the horizon and the visible hemisphere; with whole-sign houses it would be an approximation, so left out.
- Adhi yoga's seven sorts and the planets' strength (Badarayana's note) are shown in the More view only.
