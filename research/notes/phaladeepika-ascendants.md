# The rising sign (Phaladeepika 9.1-13) and Brihat Jataka's matching text

**Source:** Mantreswara's *Phala Deepika*, tr. G. S. Kapoor (in copyright), pp. 105-107, read in full. **File:** `data/rules/phaladeepika-ch9-ascendants.yaml`, **unchecked**, paraphrased. Topic **chart:lagna**
("what is my rising sign like"). It also shows Brihat Jataka XVII's text for the same sign (public domain, quoted), because the translator of Brihat Jataka notes that what is said of the Moon
in a sign is also said of the rising sign (and Phaladeepika 9.13 says the reverse: what is said of a rising sign holds if the Moon is in it). Each source is gated by its own file.

**Left out:** 9.14 (a planet in its exaltation sign makes a ruler of the earth): the translator himself says one exalted planet cannot do that in practice; it is already covered by chapter 7's rules.
"what is my ascendant" still gives the first-house answer; only descriptive questions ("like", "describe", "looks", "nature") reach this topic.
