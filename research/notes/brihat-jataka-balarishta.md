# Brihat Jataka VI: balarishta, early death (verses 1-12 and the commentator's counter-yogas)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 53-59, read in full; checked against the Sanskrit (BJ_06). **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-balarishta.yaml`, **unchecked**; engine `samay/engine/balarishta_bj.py`.
MOST SENSITIVE (about a baby): shown as a section of `chart:longevity`, behind the existing consent gate (stronger for a child). Counter-yogas show only beside a danger.

**Used:** 19 danger rules (6.1-6.11) and 9 of the commentator's 16 counter-yogas; a section titled "Dangers at birth, from Brihat Jataka chapter 6" before Phaladeepika-style items.

**Judgment calls (for the checker):**
- *6.11:* Iyer prints "nth, 7th, 9th, 12th, 1st or 8th"; the Sanskrit gives the 5th (suta). The 5th is used.
- *6.1 twilight:* needs the Sun on the horizon; approximated as the Sun in the 1st or 7th house. "Malefic planets in the last navamsas" is read as all of Sun, Mars and Saturn.
- *6.6 planetary fight:* "defeated by a malefic" is approximated by being within a degree of one; the translator's list of who loses is not computed.
- *6.2:* the two pairs (12th+2nd; 6th+8th) are two rules, as the text; Garga's four pairs and the commentators' single-yoga reading are not coded.
- *Counter-yogas coded:* Jupiter powerful in the lagna; strong lagna lord unafflicted and benefically aspected; Moon in the 6th in a benefic's drekkana; full Moon between benefics; powerful benefic in a kendra; full Moon or day/waning or night/waxing; full Moon aspected by Jupiter in a kendra; Rahu in the 3rd/6th/11th aspected by a benefic; all planets in Sirodaya signs. "Powerful" = own, moolatrikona or exaltation.
- *The counter-yogas are shown beside every danger, not matched to it* (the commentator does not say which cancels which).

**Left out:** 6.9's eclipsed Sun/Moon rules (no eclipse computed); 6.12 (the time of death by the Moon's return) is in the file as the timing note, not shown; counter-yogas 4 (as 7), 10, 12, 15 (planetary fight won) and 16 (full Moon aspected by all planets); the Yavaneswara and Mandavya notes.
