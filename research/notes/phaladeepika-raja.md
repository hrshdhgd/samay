# Phaladeepika chapter 7: raja yogas and Neechabhanga

**Source:** Mantreswara's *Phala Deepika*, tr. G. S. Kapoor (in copyright), pp. 83-91, read in full. **File:** `data/rules/phaladeepika-ch7-raja.yaml`, **unchecked**, paraphrased.
Shown inside `chart:yogas`, gated separately from the other yoga sources.

**Used:** 30 of the chapter's combinations (31 entries in the file; verses 1, 3-13, 17-22, 24-25) plus the five Neechabhanga kinds (26-30), computed per debilitated planet.
Each is coded; its `definition` field says in words what the code tests.

## Judgment calls
- **"Full Moon"** (7.7, 7.11, 7.12, 7.17, 7.22): within 36° of opposition to the Sun (144°-216° elongation). The book says "brilliant white"; no measure is given.
- **Digbala** (7.4): by whole-sign house (Sun and Mars 10th, Moon and Venus 4th, Mercury and Jupiter 1st, Saturn 7th); the book measures from the middle of the house.
- **"Strong"** = own, moolatrikona or exaltation sign. **"Aspect"** = the full sights of Brihat Jataka II.13. **"Friendly"** from Brihat Jataka chapter 20.
- **7.6** (garbled): lagna lord vargottama, in a kendra or trikona, in an own or exaltation sign. **7.24(1)**: malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn) in each of the 3rd, 6th and 11th from the lagna only.
- **7.25**: one of the lords of the 11th, 9th and 2nd in a kendra from the Moon, and Jupiter ruling the 2nd, 5th or 11th.
- **Neechabhanga:** any one of the four conditions cancels (the translator, Shastri and Jataka Parijata); Ojha wanted both of the first two. The translator's notes 1 and 4 are the same condition and are merged. The "planet exalted in that sign" follows the translator (the Sun for Saturn in Aries).

## Left out
7.2 (a royal family without Duryoga or combust planets: the bot does not know a person's family), 7.14 and 7.16 (ambiguous in the translation), 7.15 (the translator calls it confusing and gives two readings),
7.23 (the Moon in the navamsa of an *adhimitra*, which the book does not define for the bot). The "king" language is kept as the book's; the frame line says it means unusual standing.
