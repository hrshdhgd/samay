# Brihat Jataka XXI: the planets in the several vargas (verses 1-10)

**Source:** N. Chidambaram Iyer (tr.), *Brihat Jataka*, Madras 1885, public domain, pp. 186-191, read in full. **File:** `data/rules/brihat-jataka-vargas.yaml`, **unchecked**; quoted, with the translator's notes where they define a term.
Topic `chart:vargas` ("what do my vargas say", "my planets' dignity"), shown with verse numbers on More.

**Used:** the number of the seven planets in their own signs, in friendly signs, and in inimical or depression signs (21.1-2); a single exalted planet aspected by a friend, and an exalted planet with a friend (21.2); an Aquarius rising sign (21.3, Satyacharya);
malefics in the solar horas of odd signs and benefics in the lunar horas of even signs (21.4-5); the Moon's drekkana, its lord's friendship and the Sarpa, Ayudha, Chatushpad and Pakshi kinds (21.6); the rising navamsa, with the vargottama case making the person chief of the class (21.7);
a planet in its own trimsamsa and the Sun or Moon in another planet's (21.8-10). Hora, drekkana and trimsamsa definitions are from chapter I (stanzas 7 and 11).

**Judgment calls (for the checker):**
- *Which planets are "malefic" and "benefic" in 21.4-5:* all three of Sun, Mars, Saturn, or all three of Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, the Moon left out. The text does not say. A mixed group shows nothing.
- *The moderate cases of 21.5:* "horas as stated but signs different" is read as every planet in the stated hora; the case the translator says the author missed (a malefic in a lunar hora of an odd sign, a benefic in a solar hora of an even sign) as every planet in the stated kind of sign, as he infers. The "both different, effects fail" case shows nothing.
- *"Friendly":* the natural friendships of chapter II (as in the strength code), not temporary friendship at birth; the translator's note to 21.6 says "at the time of birth", which would add temporary friendship.
- *"A single planet" exalted in 21.2:* exactly one exalted planet for the aspected-by-a-friend rule; any exalted planet for the accompanied rule ("one of the planets"). A friend's aspect uses the full aspects of II.13.
- *Own sign for the count in 21.1:* the planet rules the sign; the Moon in Taurus (exaltation, not rule) is not counted as own. Planets in depression count with the inimical ones.
- *Rising navamsa and vargottama:* the navamsa sign of the ascendant; vargottama when it is also the rising sign (the translator's notes a-b).
- *Left out:* the rising dwadasamsa remark ("the same effects as the rising signs", 21.7) and the Yavana/Vishnugupta dispute over an Aquarius dwadasamsa (21.3).
