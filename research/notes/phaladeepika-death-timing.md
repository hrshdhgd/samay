# Death-timing conditions (Phaladeepika 13.18-19, 20.32, 20.40)

Built at the owner's request (2026-11-13), behind its own warning and tap. **File:** `data/rules/phaladeepika-death-timing.yaml`, **unchecked**, paraphrased. Topic
**chart:death_timing** ("when will I die"). It shows, for the next five years, the stretches of days in which each of the book's conditions holds, and says first that the book
names no date and no one can foretell a death. A child gets the strongest warning (naming the child) before anything is computed.

**Rules:** the Saturn point (sum of the Sun, Moon, Jupiter and Saturn longitudes: Saturn in that sign), the afflicted lagna lord's transit through the 6th, 8th, 12th or lagna (from
the lagna and the Moon sign), Jupiter's transit through the 8th lord's sign, navamsa or their trikonas, the dashas of powerful 2nd/7th lords and occupants (maraka), and the
antardasha of a very weak 8th or 12th lord. **Judgment calls:** 13.18 applies only to "a native of short life"; the bot cannot tell and says so in the line. The book's "7 degrees"
of the Saturn point is taken as the whole sign. 13.19's navamsa-lagna and decanate-lagna versions are not used. "Powerful" (20.40) = own, moolatrikona or exaltation sign;
"very weak" = debilitated or combust. **Left out:** 20.31 (the weakest of Saturn, Mandi, Rahu, the 22nd-decanate lord, the 8th lord and navamsa lords: Mandi and the 22nd decanate
are not computed) and the Chapter 17 transit rules, which I have not read.
