# Phaladeepika chapter 14, diseases (verses 1-11)

**Source:** Mantreswara's *Phala Deepika*, tr. G. S. Kapoor (in copyright), pp. 137-140 (PDF 136-139). **File:** `data/rules/phaladeepika-ch14-health.yaml`,
**unchecked**, paraphrased. Topic **chart:health** ("what diseases might I have"). Every answer opens with the book's caution and ours: an old text's
list of what the planets stand for, **not a diagnosis, not medical advice**. This is one of the sensitive methods the owner chose to include (2026-11-12).

**Used:** verse 1, the sources of disease (planets in the 6th, 8th and 12th, the 6th lord, planets joined to it); verses 2-9, what each of the nine planets
and Gulika stands for (Gulika not computed); verse 1's rule that a trouble named by two or more independent sources may be predicted (shown as
"where they agree", by matching tags I assigned to each trouble: a judgment call, flagged below); verses 10-11, fourteen specific combinations (eye,
ear, stomach, rectal, private parts, the occupants of the 6th and 8th, carbuncle, spleen, the waning watery Moon).

**Judgment calls.** "Aspected" = the full sights of Brihat Jataka II.13; "joined" = same sign; the eye rule's right/left eye comes from the translator's note;
the ear rule reads "the 3rd and 11th houses and Jupiter, joined or aspected by Mars or Saturn" as: Mars or Saturn in or aspecting each of those three;
the rectal rule's "with a malefic" counts the Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu; the tags that link troubles across planets (bile, fever, eye, skin...) are mine.
