# The strength of the houses (Phaladeepika 15)

**Source:** Mantreswara's *Phala Deepika*, tr. G. S. Kapoor (in copyright), pp. 145-153. **File:** `data/rules/phaladeepika-ch15-house-strength.yaml`, **unchecked**,
paraphrased. Topic **chart:houses** ("how strong are my houses"). Each of the twelve houses gets the book's findings (➕ helps / ➖ harms / • note) in
plain words, with verse numbers on More. It is the tool the book uses for every life-area question, so the other answers can now be read with it.

**Used:** 15.2 (the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, 10th from the house held by benefics or by malefics), 15.3 (the lord in the 8th, combust, debilitated or in an enemy's sign
with no benefic help; a planet in the house in that state), 15.4 (a malefic in the 6th, 8th, 12th from it), 15.5 (the lord in a dusthana, or a dusthana lord in the
house; saved by a benefic), 15.9-10 (the lagna lord in the house, joined with or aspecting its lord, owning a dusthana), 15.18-19 (benefics and malefics in a house, and in
the 6th, 8th, 12th where the book reverses it).

## Judgment calls
"Three or more" for 15.2: the book says "adequate" if those houses are "occupied by auspicious planets"; I require at least three and no opposing planet. "Benefic" =
Mercury, Jupiter, Venus (and the waxing Moon in 15.2); "malefic" = Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu. "Enemy's sign" and "combust" come from Brihat Jataka chapter 20.
"Helped" = a benefic in the same sign or aspecting it by the full sights of II.13. 15.19's benefic-in-a-dusthana line is shown as a note, not as help.

## Left out
- **15.6** (a house, its lord and its karaka all without strength) and **15.25**: the karaka list in 15.17 as printed has eleven entries for twelve houses, so which karaka
  belongs to which house is not certain; not used.
- **15.7-8, 15.12** (the dashas that harm or help a house, the 22nd decanate, Mandi, ashtakavarga zero points), **15.11** (a planet owning two houses), **15.13-14**
  (bhava sandhi): not built. 15.20-24 (reading another person's house by counting from their karaka): the people feature may use it later.
